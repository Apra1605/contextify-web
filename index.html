How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

From selection to understanding in seconds. Contextify integrates seamlessly into your browsing workflow.

1

Select & Activate

Highlight any text on any webpage. A small, unobtrusive Contextify icon will appear right beside your selection.

2

Choose Your Depth

Click the icon to open a compact popup. Choose a "Quick Explain" for a concise summary or preview the "Full Story" for a deeper dive.

3

Explore the Narrative

The "Full Story" opens in a dedicated page, complete with keyword extraction, image suggestions, and simple visualizations for a rich learning experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to reach out.

Is Contextify free to use?

Absolutely. Contextify is completely free and open source. We believe in accessible knowledge for everyone. You can view the entire source code on our GitHub repository.

What browsers are supported?

Contextify is built for Chromium-based browsers. It works perfectly on Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and other browsers that support the Chrome Extension ecosystem.

How does Contextify handle my privacy and data?

Your privacy is paramount. The text you select is sent to a remote chat-completion API for processing, but we do not store your queries or personal data. The extension only manages short-lived session state to function, which is cleared regularly.
